# Changelog

All notable changes to **Istikhara** will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18
### Added
- **Bilingual PWA & Static SEO**: Full `/ur/` (default) and `/en/` routes with canonical/hreflang pairs, JSON-LD, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, and `llms-full.txt`.
- **Sunnah Istikhara Flow**: Prepare (wudu + Sahih al-Bukhari 1162 dua + device-only intention), Focus (breathing ring + tasbih beads), Result (verse card, audio recitation, Urdu tafsir, A4 PDF, share images & QR deep link).
- **9:16 Verse Video Maker**: Vertical 1080×1920 MP4 export with Arabic text, Urdu translation/tafsir, and recitation credit.
- **Native Apps (Capacitor Android & iOS)**: Offline-bundled Android & iOS shells with SplashScreen, StatusBar, Haptics, native Share, Gallery export, local daily reminders, Android back-button handling, and App/Universal Links.
- **Policy-Safe Monetization**: AdMob (Android/iOS) with UMP + ATT consent, `G` content rating, muted audio, 24h ad-free reward, and strict forbidden-zone guards; consent-gated AdSense on web; `ads.txt` & `app-ads.txt`.
- **Store Readiness**: Google Play Data Safety & Apple App Privacy docs, bilingual store listings, automated Playwright audit & store screenshot scripts, and Codemagic CI workflow.
