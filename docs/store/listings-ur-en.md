# Store Listings — Istikhara (Urdu & English)

## English Listing

- **App Name**: Istikhara — Quranic Reflection
- **Short Description (80 chars)**:
  `Perform Sunnah Istikhara & reflect on authentic Quranic verses with Urdu tafsir.`
- **Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars)**:
  `Sunnah Istikhara & Quran`
- **Full Description**:
  Istikhara is a calm, sacred, distraction-free companion for performing the Sunnah Istikhara prayer (two rak'ahs and the Masnoon Dua from Sahih al-Bukhari 1162) and reflecting upon authentic Quranic verses.

  **Key Features:**
  - **Sunnah Preparation & Dua**: Guided steps with the complete Sahih al-Bukhari 1162 Istikhara dua in Arabic, Urdu, and English.
  - **Calm Focus Mode**: Breathing geometric ring and gentle tasbih beads to center your heart before reflection.
  - **Authentic Quran Text & Translations**: Arabic (`quran-uthmani`), Urdu (`ur.jalandhry`), and English (`en.sahih`), plus audio recitations by Mishary Alafasy, Abdul Basit, and Al-Husary.
  - **Urdu Tafsir & Verse Details**: Read authentic word-for-word Urdu tafsir with clear source attribution.
  - **9:16 Verse Video Maker**: Export vertical 1080×1920 MP4 verse videos with recitation and Urdu translation/tafsir for Reels, Shorts, and WhatsApp Status.
  - **PDF & Share Cards**: Save illuminated A4 PDFs or 1080×1350 / 1080×1920 share images directly to your gallery.
  - **100% Private**: No login, no account. Your intention notes and reflection journal never leave your device.
  - **Works Offline**: Download the Quran dataset once for complete offline access.

  *Note: Istikhara verses are presented purely for reflection. The app never labels any verse "good" or "bad" or tells you "do / don't".*

- **Keywords (iOS, 100 chars)**:
  `istikhara,quran,dua,sunnah,urdu,tafsir,islamic,prayer,muslim,reflection,surah,ayah,video`
